Future Forward Desert City

Smart ideas · Clean energy · A greener tomorrow

What if a desert city ran on sun and wind, reused every drop of water and grew its own food? This is our model of how that could work.

Our vision

“To build a clean, green and self-sufficient city in the desert using renewable energy, smart technology and sustainable resources.”

Deserts cover over 20% of Earth’s land, and as the planet warms, learning to live in dry places matters more every year. We built this model to show how careful planning can turn harsh land into a place people want to live.

  • Powered by the sun and wind
  • Water that gets used again and again
  • Nothing wasted

A sustainable oasis

Old desert building tricks, like shade and airflow, working together with new technology.

Why it matters

“It helps conserve water, reduces carbon emissions, creates a healthier environment and ensures a better future for generations to come.”

Saves water

Dry regions run short of water. Recycling used water means far less is lost.

Cuts emissions

Solar panels and wind turbines replace fossil fuels, so less greenhouse gas goes into the air.

Healthier days

Cleaner air, fresh local food and green parks make a city easier to live in.

Looks ahead

What we build now decides what our children inherit.
