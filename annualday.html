From sunlight to harvest

How the system works

Several connected parts help the greenhouse use land, water and energy efficiently.

Capture sunlight

Solar panels generate electricity while an adjustable canopy helps manage the light reaching the crops.

Monitor plant growth

Light sensors track greenhouse conditions and help guide adjustments to the canopy.

Collect and reuse water

A water-recovery loop collects and filters water so it can be reused for irrigation.

Check performance

A combined dashboard displays food production, energy output and water recovery.

Components to build

  • Greenhouse or crop-growing zone
  • Adjustable solar-panel canopy
  • Light sensors or light-scale indicators
  • Water-recovery system
  • Irrigation line
  • Energy-output indicator
  • Crop-productivity index
  • Combined optimisation dashboard
